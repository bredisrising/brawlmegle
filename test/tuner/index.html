How to do each move

Punch (straight)
From your guard (fists by your chin), snap one fist straight at the screen, then bring it back.
Hook
Swing a fist around from the side, across toward the middle, elbow bent.
Uppercut
Dip the fist a little, then drive it upward, elbow bent.
Block
Both fists up in front of your face, and hold them there.
Duck
Drop your head and shoulders quickly (sitting: bend forward).
Dodge
Lean your head and shoulders quickly to one side.
Standing only
Kick or knee: lift a knee fast. Jump: a small bounce is enough.

Sitting at your laptop or standing back both work. Turned about 45° (fighting stance) reads straight punches far better than square-on. Every limb works the same way: any fast burst away from where it rested is a strike. Bounce to jump, drop to duck, lean to dodge, hands up by your face to guard.
